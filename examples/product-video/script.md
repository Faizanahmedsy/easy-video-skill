# Tidy: product video script (no voice)

A made-up task app. Every name and number on screen is dummy data.
Length: 21.5 s, 16:9. Music and on-screen text only.

| Time | Shot | On screen | Move |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0 – 3.4 | The problem | "Your tasks live in ten places." Cards from mail, chat, docs and a calendar float in depth. | The cards collapse into one point. |
| 3.4 – 5.0 | The product | The Tidy logo lands. "One calm list for every task." | The app window rises; the logo flies into its top bar (a match cut). |
| 5.0 – 9.9 | Plan | "Plan the week in one click." The cursor clicks Auto-plan; the tasks sort, one is ticked, a toast confirms. | The camera leans in. Whip pan out. |
| 9.9 – 13.8 | Ask | "Not sure what is next? Just ask." The assistant answers and lists three steps. | Dive into the first step. |
| 13.8 – 17.7 | The numbers | "Teams finish more, every week." Three rolling number tiles and a line chart (sample data). | A cobalt wave wipe. |
| 17.7 – 21.5 | End card | Tidy. "Every task in its place." Start free. tidy.example | A cursor clicks the button. |
